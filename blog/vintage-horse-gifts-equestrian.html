Vintage Gifts for Horse Lovers — A Value Pick & a Splurge

November 2026
← Back to Journal

Christmas is a month away, and horse lovers are some of the easiest people to shop for in vintage. There is a lot of equestrian art, jewelry and decor out there, and much of it costs less than a new gift would. Below are ten vintage gifts for horse lovers across five categories: horse art, equestrian jewelry, a horse picture frame, a horse head wall hook and horse bookends. Where the set allows, each category has a value pick and a splurge.

Prices run from $18 to $449.99. Vintage pieces come from individual sellers, so order early and check each seller’s shipping time if you want a gift under the tree by December 25.

The best horse lover gifts are the ones she can hang, wear or put on a shelf where the horse is always in view.

Vintage horse gifts — common questions

What are good vintage gifts for horse lovers?

Framed horse prints, sterling silver horse rings, horse head coat racks, horse bookends and horse-themed picture frames are all good vintage gifts for horse lovers. Many are available for under $100.

What is a good Christmas gift for someone who loves horses?

A framed vintage horse print, a sterling silver equestrian ring or a pair of horse bookends all make good Christmas gifts for a horse lover. A horse picture frame under $20 works as a stocking stuffer.

Are vintage horse bookends worth buying?

Vintage horse bookends are popular with both collectors and decorators. Check the material, the condition and whether the listing includes both bookends in the pair, since a single one is worth much less.

How can I give a vintage gift in time for Christmas?

Order early and check the seller's handling and shipping time on the listing. Vintage items ship from individual sellers, so delivery times vary.
